Peer Class Visits

Ten minutes, once a month, in a colleague's room. Not an evaluation — just a chance to see good teaching happen and steal an idea or two.

The best PD we have is right down the hall. The idea is simple: once a month, spend ten minutes in a colleague's classroom. Slip in, watch what's happening, and slip out. No forms, no rubric, no write-up — just curiosity and a quick thank-you afterward.

Pick one person to start with, set yourself a gentle monthly reminder, and jot one line in the shared log so we can all see the visits adding up. That's it.
